About Me

I'm Moaza, a Computer Science student and web developer who likes turning ideas into interfaces people actually enjoy clicking around in. Right now I split my time between two internships: at Code Alpha I focus purely on the frontend, obsessing over the small details that make an interface feel alive, and at Code Saviours I work full stack, building real features from the database all the way to the browser.

Outside of internships, I'm sharpening my fundamentals in Python and C++, and I learn best by building every project on this page started as "I wonder if I can make this." I care about clean code, tidy UI, and the kind of small interaction detail most people scroll past without noticing.

When I'm not writing code, I'm usually reading up on new frontend tricks, redesigning this very portfolio for the third time, or figuring out how to make a button feel just a little more satisfying to click.
